Match Fit privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Match Fit is a game for iPhone in which your real workouts, read from Apple Health, play a made-up football career. It is made by Sam Johnson (Samuel Edward Johnson), an independent developer in the UK ("we", "us").

In short: your health data stays on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no adverts, so we never receive your health data or anything else about you.

What Match Fit reads from Apple Health

With your permission, Match Fit reads these from Apple Health:

  • Workouts: the type (for example a run or a strength session), when it started, how long it lasted, and whether you entered it by hand.
  • Heart rate recorded during your workouts.
  • Distances for walking, running, cycling and swimming.
  • Workout effort scores, where your device records them.

Match Fit only reads. It never writes anything to Apple Health, and it does not read anything else, such as your weight, sleep or medical records.

You choose what Match Fit can see when you first connect Apple Health, and you can change it at any time in iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Match Fit.

What we do with it

Match Fit uses your workouts only to play the game on your iPhone. It compares each workout with your own usual effort to rate your match performances, build your player's stats and play your fixtures.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Where your information is kept

Everything Match Fit knows is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. That covers your career, past careers, settings and a summary of the workouts behind each game.

  • No servers. Match Fit has no servers and no accounts. It does not send your information over the internet.
  • No analytics, adverts or tracking. Match Fit contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no third-party code that could see your data.
  • Your iPhone's own backups. Like any app's data, your career is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups if you have them switched on. Apple handles these under its own privacy policy, and we can't see them.

Backups you make yourself

In Settings you can save a backup file of your careers, including the summary of the workouts behind each game. The file is only created when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes, such as the Files app. Once saved, it is like any other file of yours: keep it somewhere safe, and only share it with people you trust.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Match Fit schedules a few reminders on your iPhone, such as when your week in review is ready. When a new workout arrives in Apple Health, Match Fit may read it in the background, play the games it covers and tell you the results. All of this happens on your iPhone: notifications are created there and never sent through a server.

Test versions (TestFlight)

If you are testing a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may share with us:

  • the name and email address you joined with;
  • feedback and screenshots you choose to send;
  • crash reports and basic device information, such as the iPhone model and iOS version.

These come under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix problems and improve the game, and they never include your health data.

Test versions also have a Send my progress button in Settings. It shows you a plain-text summary of your career's game numbers (such as your club, division, league position, appearances, goals and ratings) and lets you send it however you choose, for example by email or message. It never includes your name, your workouts or any health data, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements to share crash data with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. They don't include your health data. You can switch this off at any time.

Deleting your information

Deleting the Match Fit app deletes everything it has stored. Your workouts stay in Apple Health, which Match Fit never changes. Starting a new career in the app keeps your old one under Past careers on your iPhone. Removing the app removes those too.

Children

Match Fit is not aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because we never receive your personal information, there is nothing about you for us to hand over, correct or delete; it all stays on your iPhone under your control. If you have a question about your privacy, contact us using the details below. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your health data, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
