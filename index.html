Run of Form privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Run of Form is a game for iPhone in which your real workouts, read from Apple Health, play a made-up football career. It is made by Sam Johnson (Samuel Edward Johnson), an independent developer in the UK ("we", "us").

Run of Form was called Match Fit until November 2026. Only the name changed: this policy covers both.

In short: your health data stays on your iPhone. If you choose to, you can share anonymous game stats with us; nothing else about you is ever sent. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

What Run of Form reads from Apple Health

With your permission, Run of Form reads these from Apple Health:

  • Workouts: the type (for example a run or a strength session), when it started, how long it lasted, and whether you entered it by hand.
  • Heart rate recorded during your workouts.
  • Distances for walking, running, cycling and swimming.
  • Workout effort scores, where your device records them.

Run of Form only reads. It never writes anything to Apple Health, and it does not read anything else, such as your weight, sleep or medical records.

You choose what Run of Form can see when you first connect Apple Health, and you can change it at any time in iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Run of Form.

What we do with it

Run of Form uses your workouts only to play the game on your iPhone. It compares each workout with your own usual effort to rate your match performances, build your player's stats and play your fixtures.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Where your information is kept

Everything Run of Form knows is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. That covers your career, past careers, settings and a summary of the workouts behind each game.

  • No accounts. Run of Form has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or anything to sign in with.
  • No adverts or tracking. Run of Form contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics code, and nothing that could see your health data.
  • Your iPhone's own backups. Like any app's data, your career is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups if you have them switched on. Apple handles these under its own privacy policy, and we can't see them.

The only thing that can leave your iPhone is the anonymous game stats described next, and only if you say yes.

Game stats you choose to share

At the end of setup (or once, later, if you started before this was added), Run of Form asks whether you'd like to share anonymous game stats to help us improve the game. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Game stats.

What's sent, if you say yes:

  • A random code made by the app when it was installed. It isn't linked to your Apple ID, your iPhone, your name or anything else about you, and reinstalling the app makes a new one.
  • The app's version, and how many weeks old your career is.
  • Game numbers, such as: the workouts a week you planned, how many games you played and workouts you did each week, your season, division, league position and squad status, which setup screens you reached, which parts of the game you opened, and your answers to the game's questions (for example yes or no to the manager's ask).

Because the number of games and workouts you did each week reflects how often you trained, we treat it as fitness information and only send it with your yes.

What's never sent: your name or anything you typed, your nationality or shirt number, workout types, times, lengths, distances, heart rate, effort or pace, any other health data, calendar dates or times of day, your location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Where it goes: to our own server, hosted for us by Cloudflare, with the data stored in the European Union. Cloudflare runs the server and doesn't use the data for anything else. The server doesn't keep your IP address. We use the stats only to understand and improve the game (for example, how many people keep playing after a month). They are never sold, never shared with anyone else and never used for advertising.

How long: stats are deleted automatically after 24 months.

Seeing and deleting them: Settings > Game stats shows the last things sent from your iPhone, in plain words. "Delete my game stats" deletes everything sent from your iPhone from our server and starts again with a new random code.

Backups you make yourself

In Settings you can save a backup file of your careers, including the summary of the workouts behind each game. The file is only created when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes, such as the Files app. Once saved, it is like any other file of yours: keep it somewhere safe, and only share it with people you trust.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Run of Form schedules a few reminders on your iPhone, such as when your week in review is ready. When a new workout arrives in Apple Health, Run of Form may read it in the background, play the games it covers and tell you the results. All of this happens on your iPhone: notifications are created there and never sent through a server. Whether notifications are on is one of the game stats sent if you've said yes.

Test versions (TestFlight)

If you are testing a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may share with us:

  • the name and email address you joined with;
  • feedback and screenshots you choose to send;
  • crash reports and basic device information, such as the iPhone model and iOS version.

These come under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix problems and improve the game, and they never include your health data.

Test versions also have a Send my progress button in Settings. It shows you a plain-text summary of your career's game numbers (such as your club, division, league position, appearances, goals and ratings) and lets you send it however you choose, for example by email or message. It never includes your name, your workouts or any health data, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements to share crash data with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. They don't include your health data. You can switch this off at any time.

Deleting your information

Deleting the Run of Form app deletes everything it has stored on your iPhone. If you shared game stats, use Settings > Game stats > Delete my game stats first to remove them from our server too; otherwise they're deleted automatically after 24 months. We can't find them for you ourselves, because they're only labelled with the random code on your iPhone. Your workouts stay in Apple Health, which Run of Form never changes. Starting a new career in the app keeps your old one under Past careers on your iPhone. Removing the app removes those too.

Children

Run of Form is not aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Your health data and career stay on your iPhone under your control. The anonymous game stats you may share aren't linked to you, so we can't pick out yours, but you can delete them yourself at any time in Settings > Game stats. If you have a question about your privacy, contact us using the details below. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your health data, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
